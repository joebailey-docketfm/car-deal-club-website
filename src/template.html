ABOUT US

Car buying without the guesswork

Car Deal Club is a UK focused motoring membership club. We help you make more informed car buying decisions and save money for as long as you own your car.

Why we started

Buying a car is one of the biggest purchases most people make, and it is easy to feel out of your depth. Adverts leave things out, finance quotes are hard to compare and extras get added at the last minute.

Car Deal Club puts someone in your corner. Send us an advert and we will help you check the price, spot potential red flags, understand the numbers and prepare your questions before you buy.

Savings that keep going

We work with dealer and motoring partners to negotiate member-only discounts, on buying a car and on running it: servicing, tyres, valeting, parts and seasonal checks.

What members get

Exclusive deals, offers and discounts
